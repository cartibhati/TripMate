# TripMate AI: Multi-Agent Travel Planner

## intro
Meet TripMate AI: LangGraph, FastAPI, and Groq LPU.

## architecture
An input guardrail filters prompts, while the supervisor routes flight, hotel, and Open-Meteo weather agents in parallel into a budget analyst.

## hitl
Human-in-the-loop pauses the draft for review: approve to finalize or request revisions, backed by PostgreSQL memory.
